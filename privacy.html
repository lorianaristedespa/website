Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-02

This policy covers Lorian Personal Assistant, a private AI assistant operated for personal email and calendar assistance. This informational website does not collect Google account data or offer public registration.

Data accessed

With Google OAuth authorization, the assistant can access email content, sender and recipient addresses, subjects, attachments when needed, message metadata and labels in its authorized mailbox. It can also access calendar events, titles, times, locations, descriptions, and attendee details on calendars available to that Google account.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored on the assistant’s host to maintain the authorized connection. Google account passwords are not required for this API connection.

How data is used

Data is used to carry out requested assistance: email summaries, relevant-message notifications, drafting and sending approved messages, inbox organization, scheduling, and calendar briefings. Relevant information may be retained in assistant notes and conversation history for continuity.

Processing and disclosure

The assistant runs on hosted infrastructure. Relevant email or calendar information may be sent to the configured AI model provider to perform the requested task, and summaries or notifications may be delivered through a messaging service selected by the operator. Those providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

The application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google data is not published in this website or its public source repository. Any transfer is limited to providing the requested assistance, authorized delivery, security needs, or legal obligations.

Lorian Personal Assistant’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google API data must not be used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage, retention, and security

OAuth credentials and working data are stored on the assistant’s host, separately from this public website. Notes, conversation records, and operational logs may retain information needed for continuity and troubleshooting until removed by the operator or through a deletion request. Backup copies may persist until their retention cycle ends. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Control and deletion

The operator can revoke Google API access at Google Account connections, stop forwarding email, or remove calendar shares. Revocation stops future API access but does not automatically delete previously retained information.

To request deletion of retained email or calendar information, notes, or OAuth credentials, contact lorianaristedespa@gmail.com. Requests will be reviewed and handled by the operator; service providers may have separate retention requirements.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and contains no analytics scripts, forms, or application cookies. GitHub may collect technical access logs under the GitHub privacy statement.

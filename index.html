Lorian Personal Assistant

A private, owner-operated AI assistant for email and calendar help. This application is for its operator’s personal use, not a public signup service.

What the application does

Lorian uses Google APIs to read and summarize email in its dedicated Gmail account, send approved messages, and organize events on its own calendar. It also reads calendars explicitly shared with that account; those shares retain their Google access permissions.

Email access includes messages forwarded to the assistant’s inbox. Sending messages and changing calendar events require the operator’s authorization.

Google account access

Access is granted through Google OAuth consent. The application requests Gmail read, send, and modify permissions and Calendar permissions. These permissions support reading messages, managing the assistant’s inbox, and calendar assistance. They do not grant access to unrelated Google accounts.

The operator can revoke access through Google Account connections.
